# Personal Financial Planning — FPS

## Project Overview

Academic financial planning project focused on developing a goal-based personal financial plan using cash-flow analysis, risk profiling and asset allocation.

## Objective

To evaluate the financial position, priorities and risk profile of a client and develop a structured investment plan aligned with different financial goals and time horizons.

## Analysis Performed

- Analysed income, expenses and existing investments
- Assessed emergency fund adequacy
- Incorporated insurance requirements into the revised cash flow
- Conducted risk profiling
- Calculated goal-wise future values and SIP requirements
- Developed goal-based asset allocation
- Prioritised and phased financial goals based on available investment capacity

## Key Outcome

The analysis showed that the calculated monthly SIP requirement was higher than the available investment capacity.

Therefore, a priority-based and phased investment approach was developed instead of attempting to fund all goals simultaneously.

The final allocation was structured across:
- Liquid / low-risk investments for short-term goals
- Balanced growth investments for medium-term goals
- Growth-oriented investments for long-term goals
